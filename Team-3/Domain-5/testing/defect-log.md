# SwipeX - Defect Log

## Defect Tracking Process

All defects discovered during testing will be:

1. Recorded
2. Assigned severity and priority
3. Assigned to the responsible developer
4. Fixed
5. Retested
6. Closed after verification

## Defect Status

- Open
- In Progress
- Fixed
- Retest
- Closed
- Reopened

## Defect Severity

- Critical
- High
- Medium
- Low
